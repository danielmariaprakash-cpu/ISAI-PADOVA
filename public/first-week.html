Your first week in Padova

Four steps, in this order. Deadlines and fees change, so always confirm with the office or counter.

Step 1 · Day 1

📱 Buy an Italian SIM

Do this right after landing, at the airport or a phone shop (TIM, Vodafone, WindTre, Iliad, Ho.). Take your passport. You need an Italian number for the Poste kit, the Questura SMS and the university.

Step 2 · within 8 working days of arrival

📮 Collect the kit at Poste Italiane and submit it

Pick up the yellow permesso di soggiorno kit at a post office with a Sportello Amico, fill it in (see the video guide) and hand it in at the counter with your documents, such as passport and visa copies, photos, and proof of enrolment, insurance, funds and accommodation. Pay the stamp duty and fees there. Keep the receipt: it shows your Questura appointment details and the code to track your application.

Step 3

🎓 Enrol at the University student office

Go to the Student Registrar, Via Venezia 6. International students should contact the Global Engagement Office, Lungargine del Piovego 1. Email immatricolazioni.studenti@unipd.it, or call +39 049 8273131 (Mon–Fri, 9:00–17:00). You need the enrolment proof for the Questura.

Step 4 · wait

🛂 Wait for your Questura appointment

The Questura calls you or sends an SMS to the number on your kit, with a date for fingerprints. Go to Piazza Giacomo Zanellato 7 with your original documents and the Poste receipt. Later you will be told when to collect your permesso di soggiorno. Keep your phone on and check your tracking code.
